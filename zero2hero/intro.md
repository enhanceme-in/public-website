# 📚 Learning Timeline — React & React Native Mastery

## ⏳ How Much Time Will It Take if You Invest 2 Hours a Day?

This depends on your **pace**, **prior experience** (even minimal), and **consistency**.

Our curriculum takes you from **absolute basics** to **advanced React/React Native** and **real-world projects**.  
If you **practice daily for 2 hours** (coding + watching video explanations), you can realistically complete the journey in **5–8 months**.

---

## 🗂 Estimated Timeline (English)

| **Part 1**     | Foundations of Programming & Web Basics (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) | 1.5–2 months       |

| **Part 3**     | Embracing React Native (Mobile Development)                     | 1.5–2 months       |

---

### ✅ Key Factors Influencing Timeline

- **Consistency** — Daily practice beats sporadic long sessions.
- **Active Learning** — Code along, complete exercises, experiment.
- **Problem-Solving** — Debug and understand errors quickly.
- **Leveraging Resources** — Video explanations & live sessions can **shorten** the timeline.

> 💡 With dedication and our resources, this journey is absolutely achievable!
